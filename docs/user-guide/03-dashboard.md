# 3. Dashboard

[← Contents](README.md)

The Dashboard shows how the store is doing today and what needs attention. It only shows
information: nothing on it changes your data.

**Who can see it:** everyone.

## The four tiles at the top

| Tile | What it shows | Tap it to… |
|---|---|---|
| **Today's sales** | Today's sales in ₹, with the number of invoices and the average bill. Returns made today are taken off. | — |
| **Stock value** | All saleable (unexpired) stock valued at MRP, and how many medicines you have. | — |
| **Low / out of stock** | Medicines at or below their reorder level. | Open Inventory's **Low stock** view, lowest stock first. |
| **Expiring ≤ N days** | Medicines with a batch expiring inside your warning window (Settings › Inventory). | Open Inventory's **Expiring** view, soonest first. |

## Sales overview

A bar chart of your sales.

- Switch between **7D** (last 7 days), **30D** (last 30 days) and **12M** (last 12 months).
- The last bar, today or this month, is in full colour.
- Hover over (or long-press) a bar to see its date and amount.
- The heading shows the **Total**, the **Avg** and the **Peak** for the period.

## Sales by category

A ring chart of this month's sales by category: Tablets, Capsules, Syrups, Injections, OTC and
Topical. Hover over a row in the legend to highlight that category and see its share in the
centre. Otherwise the centre shows the month's total, *"Sales this month"*.

## Cards at the bottom

- **Reorder soon:** the 4 medicines closest to running out compared with their reorder level, with
  a bar such as "3 / 20 min". **View all** opens Inventory.
- **Expiring batches:** the 4 batches expiring first. Red means a month or less (or already
  **Expired**). Amber means inside your warning window.
- **Recent invoices:** the last 5 bills. Tap one to open it in Invoices.
- **Stockist dues:** the total owed to stockists, how much of it is overdue, and the 4 stockists
  with the most overdue. Tap to open **Purchases › Dues**.
- **Customer dues:** the total customers owe you ("to collect"), how much is 90+ days old, and the
  top 4 customers. Tap a customer to open their statement.
